# HER-200-Track-3-ElevateHER
**Group 2 Track 3 ElevateHER**

"Uplift" is an app/dashboard that helps women build real leadership experience. Women who have already led a team, a club or a project are more likely to step into leadership roles later. "Uplift" makes that path clear: it shows each user where she stands today and which experiences will move her forward.

--------------------
**Our approach**
--------------------

ElevateHER turns leadership growth into a clear path that you can measure:

Leadership readiness score: a progress bar from 0% to 100% shows how ready a user is for leadership opportunities.
Six core experiences: users log the experiences that build leadership, and each one moves the score forward:

&check;Being a mentor

&check;President of a club

&check;Leading a project

&check;Doing research

&check;Panel experience

&check;Internship experience

Visible progress: each logged experience shows a woman what she has already done and what to do next.

--------------------
**Upon Completion**
--------------------

Once completed, the Uplift app/dashboard will generate a certificate of completion. The user can upload it to LinkedIn along with a post stating they completed the roadmap. Once completed the user is invited to the current year's "Uplift" conference and network. The women who complete the course are eligible to become mentors for women who are new to the app/dashboard.

--------------------
**Steps for the Future**
--------------------
We will measure how well Uplift works by following the women who complete the roadmap. For each graduate we will record where she goes next, such as leadership roles, promotions, or new positions.

One year after the first group graduates, we will interview them to learn:

&check;What they achieved after finishing the program

&check;What worked well in the program

&check;What was missing that they needed

&check;What they would add for future participants

We will use what they tell us to improve Uplift for each new group.
